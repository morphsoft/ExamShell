Assignment name  : ft_swap
Expected files   : ft_swap.c
Allowed functions:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a function that swaps the contents of two integers the addresses of
which are passed as parameters.

Your function must be declared as follows:

void	ft_swap(int *a, int *b);

Examples:

With a = 1 and b = 2, after ft_swap(&a, &b) we have a = 2 and b = 1.
With a = -5 and b = 0, after ft_swap(&a, &b) we have a = 0 and b = -5.
