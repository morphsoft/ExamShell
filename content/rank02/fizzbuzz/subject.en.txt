Assignment name  : fizzbuzz
Expected files   : fizzbuzz.c
Allowed functions: write
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a program that prints the numbers from 1 to 100, each separated by a
newline.

But for multiples of 3, it prints "fizz" instead of the number.
For multiples of 5, it prints "buzz" instead of the number.
For numbers that are multiples of both 3 and 5, it prints "fizzbuzz" instead.

Examples:

$> ./fizzbuzz
1
2
fizz
4
buzz
fizz
7
8
fizz
buzz
11
fizz
13
14
fizzbuzz
[...]
97
98
fizz
buzz
$>
